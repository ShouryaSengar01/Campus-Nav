# CampusNav — Initial Project Plan

## Problem
Students, visitors, and new campus members can have difficulty locating academic blocks, facilities, and accessible paths on a large campus.

## Proposed solution
A mobile-friendly campus navigation app that helps users find campus landmarks and explore routes between them.

## Initial scope
- Search/select a campus destination
- View important campus landmarks
- Display a suggested route
- Offer walking-first navigation, with other modes considered where appropriate
- Plan for accessibility information

## Immediate next actions
1. Confirm the exact campus and landmarks to include.
2. Gather reliable map data and verify pedestrian paths.
3. Decide on mapping/routing technology.
4. Implement and test one real route.
5. Capture genuine screenshots and update the repository.

## Risks and dependencies
- Accurate route guidance depends on reliable campus map data.
- GPS accuracy may be weak indoors.
- Accessibility claims must be verified in person.
- Any real deployment should explain location-data use and request permissions clearly.
